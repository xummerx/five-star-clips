Five Star Clips Uploader

A small personal tool that uploads short gaming clips to the Five Star Clips YouTube channel. It runs only on the channel owner's own computer and is not offered to anyone else.

What it does

  • Uploads clips that passed its quality checks to the owner's own channel, using the YouTube Data API.
  • Reads the channel's own uploads back to confirm each video is processed and public.

Google permissions it asks for

  • youtube.upload: upload videos to the owner's channel.
  • youtube.readonly: see which channel is signed in and the status of its videos.

It cannot edit or delete videos, and it does not read anything outside the owner's own channel.
